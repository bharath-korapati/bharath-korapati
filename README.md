### Featured project

**[Credit-card fraud detection on imbalanced data](https://github.com/bharath-korapati/fraud-detection-imbalanced)**
XGBoost, LightGBM, anomaly detection and SHAP reason codes on 284,807 transactions (0.17% fraud).
- PR-AUC **0.805** on a time-based test set, catching 77% of fraud with 82% of alarms correct
- Showed that applying SMOTE before the split fakes a "perfect" **1.000** score (0.864 done correctly)
- Documented why a cost-tuned threshold *failed* on test, not just the numbers that looked good
